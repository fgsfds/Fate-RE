Use the two profiles in [RESTORATION-AGENTS.md](../../../docs/RESTORATION-AGENTS.md)
when independent judgment or useful overlap justifies a child. Read the selected
TOML once. The integrator runs ready scripts, deterministic checks, CI observation
and small edits directly; no worker quota.

- Assign fresh bounded context, one unresolved question, absolute cwd/runtime,
  frozen inputs, owned scratch, budget, outputs and stop rule. Review evaluation
  gets raw evidence without the intended verdict. Required outputs must exist.
- The integrator owns shared edits, acceptance, metrics and unsigned publication.
  One exclusive windowless DOSBox lease applies to parent and children. Freeze
  source/tool inputs during gates; stop at the first useful result.
- Preserve exact bytes, complete context/fixup guards, LE typing and targets,
  honest fixture scope and separate native/inline/table attribution. A focused
  review may finish while final parent gates are pending.
- Observe exact-SHA CI directly using the guide's paginated push-run/job routes.
  Retain live handles; use actual clock deadlines. Pending/absent required checks
  and observation timeouts are unverified. An empty aggregate status list is not
  a pending required check. Report extra failures and resolve their policy before
  claiming overall CI success; distinguish required-gate success from all checks.
- Compare complete outputs and end-to-end wall time, including parent corrections.
  Saved candidates do not measure fresh rewrite speed; absent token counters do
  not establish token savings.

An existing broader pause stays in effect during explicitly requested bounded
work. A new pause stops launches in its scope; observe live jobs and release the
compiler lease only after DOSBox is absent. Publication follows existing user
authorization, not an extra permission inferred from this skill.
